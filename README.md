# Repository Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines throughout the event.

### 1. Branch Rules
- The `bugbounty` and `fix` branches must **never be deleted**.
- The `bugbounty` branch will be deployed from the start of the event.
- Until the Bug Bounty round begins, keep `main` and `bugbounty` updated simultaneously and in sync.

### 2. Bug Bounty Phase — Attack
- Once the Bug Bounty round begins, **no further changes may be made to the `bugbounty` branch**.
- Hackathon development can continue on the `main` branch throughout this phase.

### 3. Fix Phase
- Once the attack phase ends, review the bugs reported during the Bug Bounty round.
- Implement the necessary fixes and push your changes to the `fix` branch.
- You will receive a heads-up before you are allowed to push your fixes for deployment.

### Important
- **You will be penalized for making any changes to another team's branches.**
- Follow the phase-wise instructions and do not modify restricted branches during the Bug Bounty round.
